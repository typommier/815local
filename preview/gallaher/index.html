Need to reschedule a pickup?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Michaela spends pickup day and the two days before it baking and packing orders. Bread that isn't picked up by the end of the last pickup window is donated, and isn't refunded.

How do I crisp the crust back up?

Run the loaf under water, then put it straight on the rack of an oven preheated to about 350° until the crust is as crispy as you like. Works for frozen, refrigerated or day-old loaves.

The baker

Meet Michaela

Gallaher Sourdough is Michaela Gallaher's microbakery, run out of her home kitchen in Plainfield since 2023. Every loaf starts with organic flour and her own organic sourdough starter.

A dedicated bread oven lets her bake up to 140 loaves in a single day, and she's still a one-person operation, shaping and scoring every loaf herself.
