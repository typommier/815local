Adoptable cat room

Come hang out. Maybe go home with a new best friend.

We foster cats and kittens for local animal control in a cage-free room set up like a living room, with couches, climbing trees, hideouts and toys galore.

  • Free to visit any time the store is open, 9am to 7pm daily
  • Cats are fixed, vaccinated and microchipped by the rescue
  • Ages 8 weeks to 12 years, 500+ adoptions so far
  • Adoption fee is $75, paid in cash to the rescue
  • Visitors under 16 need an adult with them

Other services

A little extra help

Nail trims

Megan trims nails for cats, small animals (ferrets, rabbits, guinea pigs, birds and more) and reptiles like bearded dragons and iguanas.

Heading out of town? We'll care for your rabbit, ferret, guinea pig, tortoise, turtle, bearded dragon, gecko, frog, snake and more for a small daily fee. You bring the cage or tank, food and essentials.

Meet the owner

Hi, I'm Megan.

I've spent over 25 years in the animal industry: kennels, rescues, 3+ years of pet training, and over 10 years as a veterinary technician. I hold bachelor's degrees in Animal Science and Agricultural Business from Oklahoma State.

"I know what it is like to be an owner on a budget and an owner sparing no expense! Both create long, lasting love and companionship with pets."

Bring your questions, even the weird ones. And say hi to Maggie (Congo African Grey), Mack (Military Macaw) and Dune (Uromastyx), who live at the store.
