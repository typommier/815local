What we offer

Therapy that fits the person in front of us

Our clinicians work with individuals, children, adolescents, adults, couples, families, and any combination of people looking for more comfort and clarity in their relationships.

Talk therapy

Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), dialectical behavior therapy (DBT), psychodynamic, mindfulness-based, internal family systems (IFS), play therapy for kids, sex therapy and more.

Brainspotting

A focused, trauma-informed method built on a simple idea: where we look affects the way we feel. Your clinician helps you find and process where distress is held, reaching deeper than talk therapy alone.

Offered by Sara Rivier, Taylor Krull and Brendan Keska.

EMDR

Eye movement desensitization and reprocessing, an evidence-based therapy for trauma and distressing memories.

Offered by Shafeen Fatima.

Couples & families

Support for couples and relationship issues, families, parenting struggles and big life transitions.

Kids & teens

Play therapy, emotional regulation, teen issues, and help navigating school, including IEPs and 504 plans.

Nutrition counseling

Tara Ward, RD, LDN, helps with eating disorders, diabetes, weight management, food allergies, gut health, pre- and postnatal nutrition and more.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I schedule my first appointment?

Call (815) 828-5407, text (815) 258-3228, or email admin@riviercounseling.com. Let us know you're looking for services and Todd will guide you from there, including paperwork and matching you with a clinician.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in network with Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, Health Alliance, Optum, UMR and UnitedHealthcare. Our billing coordinator verifies your benefits and bills insurance for you. Clients are ultimately responsible for the cost of therapy.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our out-of-pocket rate is $125 per session for clients who are uninsured or underinsured (as allowed by law). You can also apply to the local nonprofit Mental Health Matters Fund.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. We see clients in person in Minooka and by telehealth. Some clinicians are telehealth only.

What is Brainspotting, and how do I try it?

Brainspotting was discovered by David Grand, PhD. It works on the idea that where we look affects the way we feel, helping clients process trauma stored deeper than talk therapy alone can reach. New clients can email Todd at admin@riviercounseling.com and mention Brainspotting, and a Brainspotting clinician will reach out to see if it's a good fit. Current clients can ask their clinician about adding a few Brainspotting sessions.

Who do you work with?

Individuals, children, adolescents, adults, couples and families. Each clinician's age range is listed under Our team.

My GPS took me to an apartment building. Where are you?

That's East Mondamin. Turn around and head west. You'll cross Ridge Road, and we're shortly after, in the Mondamin Professional Center next to Grandma's Pancake House.
