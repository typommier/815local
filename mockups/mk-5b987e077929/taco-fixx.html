About Taco Fixx

Real Tex-Mex, made the right way

Taco Fixx is a locally owned Tex-Mex spot serving fresh street tacos, burritos, quesadillas, tamales, and tortilla soup, right here in Minooka.

Known around town for quality ingredients and friendly service, with a homemade funnel cake to send you off happy.
