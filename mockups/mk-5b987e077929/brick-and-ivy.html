Independently Owned

A cozy corner for great people and great coffee

Brick & Ivy is an independent coffee shop that aims to provide a cozy, vibrant place for great people and great coffee, right on the border of Channahon and Minooka.

We offer coffee, lattes, refreshers, dirty sodas, teas (iced or hot), baked goods, and breakfast and lunch options, made for lingering over as much as grabbing on the go.
