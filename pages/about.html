About 815local

Built for the 815.

815local is a hyper-local directory for Minooka, Channahon, and Shorewood, Illinois. We believe local businesses deserve a platform that actually knows their neighborhood.

Our Mission

Keep it local.

Every dollar spent at a local business stays in the community. It pays a neighbor's salary, funds a kid's soccer league, and keeps the character of our towns intact. When you search for a place to eat, a contractor to call, or a shop to browse, you deserve a directory that actually understands where Minooka ends and Channahon begins, not one built for Chicago or New York.

We built 815local because national platforms weren't cutting it for the 815. Too many great local businesses were invisible online while chain locations dominated the results. We're here to change that. Our focus is hyper-local, and our goal is simple: make it easier for people in the 815 to discover and support the businesses that make this area worth calling home.

Who We Are

A neighbor, not a corporation.

815local started because I kept running into the same problem: I'd want to find a good local spot for dinner in Minooka, or a reliable plumber in Shorewood, and I'd end up wading through national chains and outdated listings just to find something that actually existed nearby. The 815 area code covers a stretch of Illinois full of great small businesses, tight-knit communities, and people who genuinely care about where they spend their money.

So I built 815local. Yes, you'll find the chains here too, because locals shop at them every day and they're part of the fabric of these towns. But the heart of this site is the independent businesses: the family-owned restaurants, the solo contractors, the boutiques that don't have a marketing budget. Getting listed is free, full stop, because these businesses deserve to be found by the neighbors who live right around the corner.

If you have a business you'd like to see listed, a suggestion for how to make the site better, or just want to say hello, my inbox is always open.
