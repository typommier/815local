About us

A microbakery in Minooka, Illinois

Sourdough You Know is a small-batch sourdough bakery located in Minooka, Illinois. We specialize in crafting delicious sourdough breads, treats, and more using locally-sourced ingredients.

Our passion is sharing the art of sourdough baking with our community. We are a licensed and insured cottage bakery.

The Sourdough Community Cart

Discover the Sourdough Cart Experience & Support Local

At Sourdough You Know, we strive to create a warm and inviting atmosphere where customers can relax and enjoy shopping from a wide variety of local small businesses on our self-serve sourdough cart.

The cart is out every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday from 7am to 7pm, stocked with fresh baked sourdough daily, along with items from soaps, local honey, jams, wax melts, body care products, farm fresh eggs, desserts, and MORE!

Shop and use one easy payment to support local. All businesses receive 100% of their profits. We strive to support our community and work together to provide our community with the best products.
