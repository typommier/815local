Drop the two farmers market flyers in this folder:

  flyer-vendors.jpg    -> the June 7 vendor-lineup flyer
  flyer-schedule.jpg   -> the every-other-Sunday season schedule flyer

The Events page (events.html) shows them in a clickable gallery and opens
them full-size in a lightbox. Until the files are added, the gallery shows a
neat placeholder and the flyer details still appear as text on the page.

JPG or PNG both work. If you use different filenames, update the matching
src="" and data-flyer="" paths in events.html.
