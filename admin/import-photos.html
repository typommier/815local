Import Photos

Two importers, same destination. Both mirror what they find into Supabase storage so the listing renders fast and we are not dependent on the original host.
From a Website
Scrapes the homepage for <img>, og:image, and twitter:image URLs, filters out icons and theme assets, then mirrors the survivors. Takes 20-40 seconds.
From Google Places
Pulls up to 5 photos for a Place ID, then mirrors them. No Place ID saved? Click Find to auto-resolve it from the business name. Takes 10-20 seconds. Skips businesses that already have photos unless you check Overwrite.
Replace any photos already on this listing.
Fill all missing photos
Resolves a Google match for every active business that has no photos and previews it. You review the matches, then fill the ones that look right. Mismatched or out-of-town matches are left unchecked by default.
