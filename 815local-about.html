About 815local

Built for the community,
by the community.

815local is a hyper-local directory and community hub serving Minooka, Channahon, and Shorewood, Illinois. We believe local businesses deserve a platform that actually knows their neighborhood.

Our Mission

Keep it local.

Every dollar spent at a local business stays in the community — it pays a neighbor's salary, funds a Little League sponsorship, and keeps the character of our towns alive. When you search for a place to eat, get a haircut, or fix your furnace, you shouldn't have to wade through national chain results to find the person down the street who's been doing it for twenty years. We built 815local so that the businesses woven into the fabric of Minooka, Channahon, and Shorewood get the visibility they've always deserved.

Who We Are

A neighbor who
knows the neighborhood.

I've lived in the 815 my whole life. I know the pride that comes with this area code — the tight-knit feel of Minooka, the quiet growth of Channahon, the energy coming into Shorewood. And I've watched too many genuinely great local businesses go undiscovered while national directories pushed chain results to the top.

So I built 815local. No venture capital, no corporate agenda — just a straightforward platform designed to connect neighbors with the businesses that make our community worth living in. If a restaurant opened on Route 6 last month, it should be easy to find. If a plumber in Channahon has forty years of five-star word-of-mouth, that reputation should live somewhere online. That's what this is for.

We're just getting started, and every business listing, every review, and every community event shared here makes the 815 stronger. Thanks for being part of it.
