FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Three Rivers Festival weekend

Hey neighbor, Channahon's Three Rivers Festival is in full swing at Central Park and runs through Sunday: five days of carnival rides, live music on two stages, food vendors, a beer garden, bingo, a craft fair, and a Kids Zone, all free to get in. The Channahon Farmers Market closes out the weekend on Sunday. Here's what's left on the schedule, plus a local deal worth grabbing before the month is out.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Three Rivers Festival, day by day
 
Friday, August 14
5:00–11:30 PM · Central Park, 24856 W. Eames St

Carnival rides, two stages of live entertainment, food vendors, and the beer garden. Free to get in.
 
Saturday, August 15
11:00 AM–11:30 PM

The big day: full carnival, both stages going, the craft fair, a Kids Zone, food vendors, and the beer garden.
 
Sunday, August 16
1:00–8:00 PM

The final day. Rides and live music through the afternoon, with the fest wrapping up at 8 PM.
ALSO SUNDAY
Channahon Farmers Market
Sunday, August 16 · 9 AM–2 PM · Channahon Tractor Supply

A pet-friendly market with local vendors: sourdough, fresh eggs, seasonal produce, flowers, local honey, and candles. Runs every other Sunday through October.
