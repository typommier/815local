FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Three Rivers Festival weekend

Hey neighbor, Channahon's Three Rivers Festival is in full swing at Central Park and runs through Sunday: carnival rides, live music on two stages, food vendors, a beer garden, bingo, a craft fair, a Kids Zone, and a parade Sunday at noon, all free to get in. The Channahon Farmers Market closes out the weekend on Sunday. Here's what's left on the schedule, plus two local deals worth grabbing before the month is out.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Three Rivers Festival, day by day
Times can change. Check ThreeRiversFest.Info for the latest schedule.
 
Friday, August 14
5:00–11:30 PM · Central Park, 24856 W. Eames St

Opening night: carnival rides, the food midway, live music on two stages, and the beer garden. Free to get in.
 
Saturday, August 15
11:00 AM to midnight

Food Midway opens at 11 AM. The free ExxonMobil Kids Zone runs noon–5 PM with bounce houses, Miller's Petting Zoo, wax hands, and temporary tattoos, plus Bluey and Bingo noon–1:45 PM. Unlimited carnival rides are $35 all day (1 PM to close). Craft Fair 1–6 PM, Bingo 1–11 PM, and the Beer Garden opens at 1 PM ($5 wristband, 21+ after 5 PM).

On the stages
Karaoke with DJ Base  2:05–4 PM
Keigher Academy of Irish Dance  4:05–5 PM
Fine Tunes Rock Block  5–7 PM
Hairbanger's Ball  8:30–11:30 PM
 
Sunday, August 16
Parade at noon, fest to 8 PM

The Parade steps off at noon, rain or shine, from W. Liberty and Tryon, up to S. Navajo and W. Sioux, ending at Channahon Junior High. Food Midway runs to 8 PM (last chance for fest food) and the Beer Garden noon–7 PM. Three pet adoptions set up 1–5 PM: Paws Up 4 Rescue, Hopeful Tails, and Wags 2 Wishes. The free Kids Zone returns 1–5 PM with face painting and sand art, plus bubble stations 1–3 PM and the petting zoo 1–5 PM. Rides are $30 unlimited 1–5 PM (sold until 4), then a $25 Last Blast 5–8 PM.

On the stages
Dragon Training Academy  1–2 PM
T-Rexplorers  2–3 PM
Dave Dinaso's Traveling World of Reptiles  3–4 PM

Also Sunday: Wine Down Wine Tasting 2–5 PM in the Beer Garden ($20 advance, $25 at the door), Bingo to 6 PM, and the Craft Fair to 6 PM.
ALSO SUNDAY
Channahon Farmers Market
Sunday, August 16 · 9 AM–2 PM · Channahon Tractor Supply

A pet-friendly market with local vendors: sourdough, fresh eggs, seasonal produce, flowers, local honey, and candles. Runs every other Sunday through October.
