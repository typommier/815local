FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Minooka at Plainfield Central Friday, Touch-A-Truck and PAW Patrol Saturday

Hey neighbor, Minooka is on the road again Friday at Plainfield Central. Twin Rail opens for the Aces cruise in the afternoon, and Heap's keeps the hayrides rolling through the weekend. Saturday morning, Shorewood hosts Touch-A-Truck at Cene's Four Seasons Park, and Chase and Skye from PAW Patrol visit Dollinger Family Farm in Channahon that afternoon. Sunday, St. Mary's in Minooka hosts its Oktoberfest. Full rundown below.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Thursday through Sunday
 
Thursday, October 1
Minooka

Heap's Giant Pumpkin Farm is open daily 10 AM–6:30 PM at 4853 US Highway 52. Weekday admission is $9 ages 3+ (2 & under free) for playgrounds, animals, corn maze, and the walking pumpkin path. Hayrides start Friday this week. Hours & tickets.
 
Friday, October 2
Minooka / Channahon / Plainfield

Minooka at Plainfield Central, varsity 7 PM at Plainfield Central High School, 24120 Fort Beggs Drive, Plainfield. Tickets on GoFan. IHSA schedule.

Aces Friday Night Cruise at Aces Classic Cars, 502 Twin Rail Drive, from 4 PM. October Fridays run through the 30th. acesclassiccars.com.

Heap's switches to weekend admission Fridays in October ($14 ages 3+). Hayrides run about 10:30 AM–6 PM, weather permitting. Farm hours 10 AM–6:30 PM. Hours & tickets.

Dollinger Fun Friday at Dollinger Family Farm, 7420 E Hansel Rd, Channahon. Train rides and face painting 10 AM–1 PM. Farm admission and parking are free. dollingerfarms.com.
 
Saturday, October 3
Shorewood / Channahon / Minooka

Shorewood Touch-A-Truck, 10 AM–2 PM at Cene's Four Seasons Park. Free. Climb into trucks and meet the crews. Quiet hour 10–11 AM for sensitive ears. shorewoodil.gov.

Troy Fire Station Open House, 10 AM–2 PM at Troy Fire Station 2, 25454 W Seil Rd, just down the road from Touch-A-Truck. Apparatus displays, station tours, demos, games and prizes. Shorewood-Troy Library calendar.

PAW Patrol Meet & Greet at Dollinger Family Farm, 7420 E Hansel Rd, Channahon, 1–2:30 PM. Chase and Skye are visiting for photos. Free admission. The farm is open 10 AM–6 PM with free parking; train rides, hayrides, the corn maze and face painting cost extra on weekends. dollingerfarms.com.

Minooka United Methodist Church 170th Anniversary, 2–4 PM at 1210 S Ridge Rd. Open house 2–3 PM, building consecration at 3 PM, then refreshments. Public welcome. minookaumc.com.

Heap's pumpkin patch and hayrides at 4853 US Highway 52. Farm hours 10 AM–6:30 PM. Weekend admission $14 ages 3+ (2 & under free). Hayrides about 10:30 AM–6 PM, weather permitting. Hours & tickets.
 
Sunday, October 4
Minooka / Channahon

Leaves and Lattes at Four Rivers Environmental Education Center, 25055 W Walnut Lane, Channahon, 9–10:30 AM. Free naturalist-led fall hike (up to 2.5 miles) with a coffee or tea latte. Register by Friday at 815-722-9470. reconnectwithnature.org.

St. Mary's Oktoberfest, noon–6:30 PM at St. Mary's Parish Center, 300 W St. Mary's St, Minooka. German dinner with brats and Offerman's potato pancakes (1 brat $15, 2 brats $20, kids meal $5), local craft beer, a live Bavarian band, kids games, face painting and a bounce house. stmarysminooka.com.

Heap's is open 10 AM–6:30 PM with hayrides again today. Same weekend admission as Saturday. No Channahon Farmers Market this Sunday (every other week; Fall Fest is Oct 11). heapsgiantpumpkinfarm.com.
GOOD TO KNOW

U.S. 6 bridge repairs in Channahon are still underway with intermittent daytime lane closures over the DuPage River. Allow extra time. IDOT notice.

Troy Township residents can bring papers and old prescriptions to a free drive-thru shred and medication disposal Saturday, Oct 3, 9–11 AM at the Troy Township Community Center, 25448 W Seil Rd, Shorewood. Proof of residency required; up to 4 grocery bags of papers. troytownship.com.
