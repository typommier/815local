FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Halloween is Saturday, and Minooka plays Loyola Friday

Hey neighbor, Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, and Minooka, Channahon and Shorewood all picked the same trick-or-treat hours: 4–7 PM. The catch is the forecast. The National Weather Service has showers and thunderstorms likely Saturday, so pack a poncho with the candy bag. Friday is the big night before that. Minooka beat Evanston 35–21 and now heads to Loyola Academy in Wilmette for a 7 PM playoff game. Back home, it's the last Aces cruise of the year on Twin Rail Drive, the last day at Dollinger, and the last night of Heap's Haunted Corn Maze. Saturday morning, PUMPkins for Puppies at Anytime Fitness in Minooka raises money for Paws Up 4 Rescue. Sunday, clocks fall back an hour, and The Village Christian Church hosts free bingo that night in Minooka and Shorewood. Full rundown below.
 
🎃 THIS WEEKEND
Thursday through Sunday
 
Thursday, October 29
Shorewood / Minooka / Channahon

DIY Halloween Pet, 5:30–6:30 PM at Shorewood-Troy Public Library. Tweens make a spooky pet (cats, ghosts, spiders) out of pipe cleaners. It's capped at 10, so register first. Shorewood-Troy Library.

Pumpkin farms: Heap's Giant Pumpkin Farm, 4853 US Highway 52, Minooka, is open 10 AM–6:30 PM with $9 weekday admission (ages 3 and up). Dollinger Family Farm, 7420 E Hansel Rd, Channahon, is open 10 AM–6 PM with free admission and parking. Heap's hours / dollingerfarms.com.

Early voting in Minooka runs 3–7 PM tonight at Village Hall, 121 E McEvilly Rd, for Grundy County voters. More voting info in Good to Know below. Grundy County Clerk.
 
Friday, October 30
Minooka / Channahon / Shorewood

Minooka at Loyola Academy, 7 PM in Wilmette, IHSA Class 8A second round. No. 35 seed Minooka (5-5) beat Evanston 35–21 last week and now visits No. 3 Loyola (8-1). IHSA pairings.

Last Aces Friday Night Cruise of 2026 at Aces Classic Cars, 502 Twin Rail Drive, from 4 PM. acesclassiccars.com.

Dollinger's last day of the season: open 10 AM–6 PM, with Fun Friday train rides and face painting 10 AM–1 PM. Admission and parking are free. dollingerfarms.com.

Heap's charges weekend admission Friday ($14, ages 3 and up), with hayrides 10:30 AM–6 PM. It's the last night for the Haunted Corn Maze, 7–11 PM ($25), plus the $6 Flashlight Maze and $6 Moonlight Hayride, or all three for $30. The haunted maze closes if it rains, and rain chances climb after 7 PM, so check Heap's page before you drive out. Hours & tickets.

Papa Magoos Halloween Costume Party, 8–11 PM at Papa Magoos Bar & Grill, 14285 S County Line Rd, Shorewood. papamagoos.com.
 
Saturday, October 31
Halloween in Minooka / Channahon / Shorewood

Trick-or-treat hours: 4–7 PM in Minooka, Channahon and Shorewood. Storms are in the forecast, so plan on rain gear. Minooka / Channahon / Shorewood.

PUMPkins for Puppies, 10 AM–2 PM at Anytime Fitness Minooka, 2019 Ridge Rd, benefiting Paws Up 4 Rescue. There's a 10 AM pumpkin workout ($35, includes a shirt), an adoption fair, vendors, and 50/50 and basket raffles. Dog food and supplies are welcome as donations. Eventbrite.

Heap's last day of the season. Hayrides run, but Heap's site lists two different closing times for Saturday (2:30 PM on the calendar, 5 PM on the hours page), so go early or call ahead. Hours / Calendar.

Last early voting session in Minooka, 8 AM–noon at Village Hall, 121 E McEvilly Rd, for Grundy County voters. Grundy County Clerk.
 
Sunday, November 1
Minooka / Shorewood

Fall back. Daylight saving time ends at 2 AM Sunday, so set clocks back an hour Saturday night. NIST.

Bingo at The Village, 6:30–7:30 PM at The Village Christian Church's Minooka campus (8965 S Bell Rd) and Shorewood campus (901 Shorewood Dr). Free to play, with prizes and snacks. At the end you can donate toward the church's Meal Pack event, and Reclaim3d matches gifts up to $15,000. Register at your campus. thevillagechristianchurch.com.
🦇 GOOD TO KNOW

Early voting for the Nov 3 general election ends this weekend. Grundy County voters: Minooka Village Hall, 121 E McEvilly Rd, has its last sessions Thursday 3–7 PM and Saturday 8 AM–noon, and the Grundy County Courthouse in Morris stays open through Monday. Grundy County Clerk.

Will County voters: Channahon Village Hall, 24555 S Navajo Dr, has early voting through Friday, 9 AM–noon and 1–4 PM. Shorewood residents can vote at Troy Township, 25448 W Seil Rd, 9 AM–3 PM through Friday. After that, the Will County Clerk's Office, 302 N Chicago St in Joliet, is open Saturday 9 AM–3 PM, Sunday 10 AM–4 PM and Monday 8:30 AM–7 PM. Channahon hours / Troy Township hours / Will County Clerk hours.

Driving through Joliet this weekend? Eastbound I-80 has lane closures between Wheeler Avenue and Richards Street from 10 PM Friday to 5 AM Monday, with heavy backups expected. Westbound drops to one lane from Chicago Street to Houbolt Road starting 8 PM Saturday and 7 PM Sunday, with full stops up to 15 minutes overnight, and the westbound Larkin Avenue ramps close Sunday night. Starting Monday, westbound traffic splits after Center Street for about a month: stay right if you want the Larkin exit. IDOT (eastbound) / IDOT (westbound).

In Channahon, U.S. 6 bridge work over the DuPage River still brings daytime lane closures, with completion expected by the end of the year. IDOT.

Shorewood bulk leaf pickup runs through Dec 1 (or the first snow). Rake leaves to the parkway at least 12 inches behind the curb, not into the street. shorewoodil.gov.
