🎃 Happy Halloween from 815 Weekly 🎃
 
FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Minooka at Evanston Friday, parade and Silo Session Saturday

Hey neighbor, the Halloween fun is starting a week early, and the forecast is on our side: mostly sunny and in the 70s Thursday through Sunday. Tonight, MCHS opens its fall play, “Almost, Maine.” Friday, Minooka opens the playoffs at Evanston at 7:30 PM, and the next-to-last Aces cruise fills Twin Rail Drive from 4 PM. Saturday is a full one in Minooka: Book or Treat at the library branch in the morning, the Halloween Parade at noon, and the first Silo Session at the grain elevators 5–8 PM with Buckwild Band. Shorewood-Troy Library throws its Spooktackular at noon, too. Sunday, kids trick-or-treat at Dollinger at 1 PM, Channahon United Methodist has its Trunk or Treat 3–6 PM, and Critical Grind in Shorewood holds its final-day party before the cafe closes. Full rundown below.
 
🎃 THIS WEEKEND
Thursday through Sunday
 
Thursday, October 22
Minooka / Channahon / Shorewood

“Almost, Maine”, 7 PM in the Performing Arts Center at Minooka Community High School Central Campus, 301 S Wabena Ave. The MCHS fall play, directed by Christina Duris. Tickets $10 adults, $8 students, children and seniors, on GoFan or at the door. Second show is Saturday at 7. mchs.net / GoFan.

Whispering Oaks Park public input meeting, 5 PM on site at 26501 W Old Kerry Grove, Channahon. Channahon Park District staff will share preliminary plans and take comments on what the neighborhood wants in the park. channahonpark.org.

Pumpkin farms: Heap's Giant Pumpkin Farm, 4853 US Highway 52, is open 10 AM–6:30 PM with $9 weekday admission (ages 3 and up). Dollinger Family Farm, 7420 E Hansel Rd, Channahon, is open 10 AM–6 PM with free admission and parking. Heap's hours / dollingerfarms.com.
 
Friday, October 23
Minooka / Channahon

Minooka at Evanston, 7:30 PM, IHSA Class 8A first round. No. 35 seed Minooka (4-5) visits No. 30 Evanston (5-4). The winner goes to No. 3 Loyola Academy next round. IHSA pairings.

Aces Friday Night Cruise at Aces Classic Cars, 502 Twin Rail Drive, from 4 PM. One more after this, Oct 30. acesclassiccars.com.

Dollinger Fun Friday: train rides and face painting 10 AM–1 PM at Dollinger Family Farm. dollingerfarms.com.

Heap's switches to weekend admission Friday ($14, ages 3 and up). Hayrides run 10:30 AM–6 PM. After dark, the Haunted Corn Maze runs 7–11 PM ($25, closed if it rains), plus the $6 Flashlight Maze and $6 Moonlight Hayride, or all three for $30. Hours & tickets.
 
Saturday, October 24
Minooka / Shorewood

Book or Treat, 10 AM–noon at the Three Rivers Public Library Minooka Branch, 109 N Wabena Ave. One free book per child or teen while supplies last, in the 2nd floor meeting room. No registration. Costumes encouraged. Three Rivers Library.

Minooka Halloween Parade, noon. The 17th annual parade starts at Minooka Elementary School (Wabena & Coady) and ends at MCHS Central Campus. Groups that want to march can call Village Hall at 815-467-2151. events.minooka.com.

STPL's Spooktackular, noon–2 PM in the Shorewood-Troy Public Library parking lot, 650 Deerwood Dr, Shorewood. Wear a costume. Weather permitting. Shorewood-Troy Library.

Aces Classic Cars Trunk or Treat show at 502 Twin Rail Drive, benefiting the Minooka Lions Club. Aces hasn't posted times on its site, so check before you go. acesclassiccars.com.

Minooka Silo Session, 5–8 PM downtown at the Minooka Grain Elevators. The village's first one, with Buckwild Band. events.minooka.com.

“Almost, Maine” closes at 7 PM at the MCHS Central Campus Performing Arts Center. GoFan.

Heap's is open 10 AM–6:30 PM with hayrides. Haunted Corn Maze 7–11 PM. Hours & tickets.
 
Sunday, October 25
Channahon / Shorewood / Minooka

Trick-or-Treat and Costume Contest at Dollinger Family Farm, 7420 E Hansel Rd, Channahon. Treats 1–2 PM at the 10 orange pumpkin posters, then the costume parade and contest at 2 PM. Get a number at the old red tractor to be in the running. Free. dollingerfarms.com.

Trunk or Treat Family Fall Fest, 3–6 PM at Channahon United Methodist Church, 24751 W Eames St. All are welcome. Registration is through the church's Zeffy link. Church newsletter (p. 7).

Critical Grind final-day party, all day Sunday at Critical Grind Board Game Cafe, 852 Sharp Dr, Shorewood (Sunday hours are 9 AM–6 PM). The cafe closes after this weekend. Its gaming events will keep going as pop-ups with other local businesses. criticalgrind.com.

Heap's is open 10 AM–6:30 PM with hayrides. The Haunted Corn Maze runs Sunday hours, 7–10 PM. Hours & tickets.
🦇 GOOD TO KNOW

Early voting for the Nov 3 general election is open. Shorewood residents can vote at the Troy Township Community Center, 25448 W Seil Rd, 9 AM–3 PM through Friday and again Oct 26–30. Shorewood Village Hall is not an early voting site. shorewoodil.gov.

Ridge Road in Minooka is still down to one lane south of Wildy Road on weekdays, about 7 AM to 3 PM, with temporary traffic signals. Both directions reopen after each workday. Village notice.

Heading into Joliet Saturday night? Starting at 8 PM, eastbound I-80 drops to one lane between Houbolt Road and Wheeler Avenue, and westbound drops to one lane from Richards Street to Larkin Avenue. Expect full stops of up to 15 minutes between 1 and 7 AM. All lanes reopen by noon Sunday. IDOT notice.

Black Road over I-55 in Shorewood still has the eastbound right lane closed near the bridge, plus overnight I-55 lane closures between U.S. 52 and Black Road. IDOT expects to finish in late October. In Channahon, U.S. 6 bridge work over the DuPage River still brings daytime lane closures through the end of the year. IDOT (Black Rd) / IDOT (U.S. 6).

Shorewood bulk leaf pickup runs through Dec 1 (or the first snow). Rake leaves to the parkway at least 12 inches behind the curb, not into the street. shorewoodil.gov.
