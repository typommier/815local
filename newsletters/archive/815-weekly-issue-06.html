FROM THE FRONT PORCH
A packed weekend, all three towns

Hey neighbor, there's a lot going on this weekend. Minooka has a pet-focused street fest benefiting a local animal rescue, Channahon has live music and karaoke plus a charm-making morning at the coffee shop, and Shorewood is throwing a free Back to School Bash for the kids. Add in a brand new set of courts and a freshly opened playground, and it's a full one. Here's the rundown.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Town by town, day by day
 
Friday, August 7
9:00 PM · Tru Country, Channahon

Karaoke with Cowboy Jake. Grab the mic.
 
Saturday, August 8
Shorewood & Channahon

Back to School Bash at eKids Pediatric Quick Care (1039 Brook Forest Ave, Shorewood), noon–2 PM. Free and open to the community: face painting, free snow cones, S&T Pizza, a dunk tank, and a backpack raffle, with WCCQ 98.3 Country broadcasting live on site. That night, TyJon Charlie plays Tru Country in Channahon at 9 PM.
 
Sunday, August 9
Minooka & Channahon

The Taste of Grundy & Kendall County, 949 Bell Road, Minooka, 11 AM–5 PM. A pet-focused street fest benefiting WAGS 2 Wishes Animal Rescue, with pet vendors, local food trucks, and a low-cost vaccine clinic on site. Over in Channahon, the Charm Bar at Brick & Ivy Coffee Co. (27754 Bluegrass Dr) runs 9 AM–1 PM: design your own charm jewelry over coffee, with baked goods freshly restocked.
NEW AROUND TOWN

Channahon Park District's new courts at Central Park just opened this week: 8 pickleball courts, 2 bocce courts, a sand volleyball court, and an outdoor fitness area. And Red Hawk Park in Channahon is officially open with new playground equipment. Both are worth adding to the rotation.
 
WORTH A VISIT
Knead More Dough is now online
Knead More Dough
CHANNAHON · BAKERY

Just launched their website at kneadmoredoughllc.com. Sourdough, coffee cakes, cookies, and sweets, all made and baked at Hive Kitchen in Channahon and sold in the storefront Tuesday through Saturday.
 
LOOKING AHEAD
Three Rivers Festival, August 12–16
 
Channahon's Three Rivers Festival
Central Park, 24856 W. Eames St · Free admission

Five days at Central Park: carnival rides, two stages of live entertainment (comedy, country, hip-hop, and rock), food vendors, a beer garden, bingo, a craft fair, and a Kids Zone.
