FROM THE FRONT PORCH
We're back, and sorry about last week

Hey neighbor, we missed last Friday. That's on us, and we're sorry. 815 Weekly is back this week. August wraps with cruise night at Aces, a home football game, the Brew Run 5k in Shorewood, Minooka's new CTE center opening, and the last Vintage Faire of the season on Sunday. Here's the rundown.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Town by town, day by day
 
Friday, August 28
Minooka

Aces Friday Night Cruise, 4–9 PM at 502 Twin Rail Drive. Classic cars, hot rods, and a casual cruise-in. It runs every Friday through October. That night, Minooka football hosts Neuqua Valley at 7 PM.
 
Saturday, August 29
Minooka, Shorewood & Channahon

Noon–3 PM, the MCHS Career & Technical Education Center grand opening at Central Campus, 301 S. Wabena Ave. Ribbon cutting at 12:15, then tours. All are invited.

At 4 PM, the Will County Brew Run 5k at Four Seasons Park, 25520 W Seil Rd in Shorewood. Church Street Brewing runs the beer garden after. Tone Capone's Tacos is on site. Benefits Shorewood HUGS. Walkers welcome, rain or shine. Registration goes up after tonight.

That night, Yankee Cowboy Duo at Tru Country, 25365 S Fryer St in Channahon, 9 PM.
 
Sunday, August 30
Minooka & Channahon

The Vintage Faire, 9 AM–2 PM on Mondamin Street in downtown Minooka (use 102 E. Mondamin St). Last outdoor faire of the season, and it's free. Antiques, vintage clothes, candles, food, drinks, and music. Same hours, the Channahon Farmers Market is at Tractor Supply, 26829 W Eames St. Pet-friendly, local vendors (sourdough, eggs, produce, flowers, honey, candles). Every other Sunday through October.
GOOD TO KNOW

Brew Run parking is on the grass toward the south end of Four Seasons Park. Come in from Seil Rd. Kids, strollers, and pets are welcome on the course. The beer garden is 21+ with ID.
