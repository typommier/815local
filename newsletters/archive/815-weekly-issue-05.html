FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Crossroads Fest weekend is here

Hey neighbor — it's the big one. Shorewood Crossroads Fest kicks off tonight and runs through Sunday at Cene's Four Seasons Park: three days of carnival rides, live music, a car show, a parade, and fireworks Saturday night. Rain odds are up Friday and Saturday, so pack accordingly — nothing that should keep you home. Full day-by-day schedule below, plus three new spots on the directory.

THIS WEEKEND
Crossroads Fest, day by day
 
Friday, July 31
5:00 PM – midnight

Food court 5–11:30 PM, carnival 5–10 PM (unlimited wristband 5:30–9:30 PM). On stage: Don't Matter to Me 7–9 PM, then Semple Band 9:30 PM–midnight. The free Special Needs Event runs 2–4 PM beforehand — slowed rides, lights and music turned down, "friends for the day" on hand.
 
Saturday, August 1
1:00 PM – midnight

The biggest day: carnival (wristbands 1–5 PM, again 5:30–9:30 PM), craft and vendor show plus Kids Zone 1–6 PM, the Car Show, food court until 11:30 PM. On stage: School of Rock 2–4 PM, OMT One Band-One More Time 4:30–6:30 PM, Beyond the Blond 7–9 PM. Fireworks at 9:15 PM, then Journey 2 Jovi to midnight.
 
Sunday, August 2
Parade at noon, fest until 5:00 PM

The Crossroads Parade steps off at noon at Geneva and Ravinia — theme "Party Like It's 1776!", Grand Marshal George Muentnich. Route: west on Geneva, south on S River Road, west on Seil Road, ending at Seil and Wynstone. Roads are barricaded 11 AM–2 PM, so plan parking. After: Classic Car Show, and the Midwest Bags Tournament (register 11 AM, bags fly at noon, $80 a team). Food court and carnival noon–5 PM, County Line 2–5 PM, fest closes at 5 sharp.
GOOD TO KNOW

Cash only on the grounds (ATMs on site). Anyone drinking needs a $5 wristband from either entrance or the beer ticket booth. Left at home: coolers, glass, weapons, knives, open fires, personal fireworks, bikes, pets (service animals welcome). Bags are checked at the gate.
