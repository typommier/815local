FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Labor Day weekend, and a Thursday send

Hey neighbor, 815 Weekly is moving to Thursday so you've got time to plan the weekend. This one covers Friday through Monday. Minooka hosts Oswego East Friday night, Heap's opens the sunflower field Saturday, and splash pads run through Labor Day. Sunday is quiet (no market). Village halls and the library are closed Monday.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Friday through Monday
 
Friday, September 4
Minooka

Aces Friday Night Cruise, 4–9 PM at 502 Twin Rail Drive. Same weekly cruise through October. That night, Minooka football hosts Oswego East at Central Campus, 7 PM varsity. Tickets on GoFan. Friday is also the last day to register a Channahon garage sale for the village map (sales run Sep 10–12).
 
Saturday, September 5
Minooka

Heap's Giant Pumpkin Farm opening day, 4853 US Highway 52, 10 AM–6:30 PM. Sunflower harvest tickets $12, ages 2 and under free, listed through Sep 13. Mums go on sale the same day. Pumpkin patch and hayrides wait until Sep 19. Bloom can slip, so check before you go.

Splash pads are still open this weekend (Minooka 9 AM–7 PM, Shorewood 10 AM–8 PM). Channahon Tomahawk Aquatic Center weekends 12–5 PM through Sep 7.
 
Sunday, September 6
Quiet

No Channahon Farmers Market this week (next is Sep 13). Vintage Faire's outdoor season ended Aug 30. Library branches closed. Heap's sunflowers still run if you missed Saturday.
 
Monday, September 7
Labor Day

Village halls in Minooka, Channahon, and Shorewood are closed. Three Rivers Library is closed. Parks stay open. Minooka splash pads are listed through Labor Day, 9 AM–7 PM. Shorewood splash pads through Labor Day weekend, 10 AM–8 PM.
GOOD TO KNOW

Minooka trash slides one day if the holiday hits your pickup day. Channahon Monday routes move to Tuesday.
