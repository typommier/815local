FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Minooka at Plainfield North Friday, pumpkins and market Sunday

Hey neighbor, Minooka is on the road Friday at Plainfield North. Earlier in Channahon, Dollinger runs Fun Friday in the morning and Sips, Sights & Sounds fills Four Rivers at night. Weekend hayrides keep rolling at Heap's. Sunday brings the Channahon Farmers Market and a Choir Boosters car show at the high school. Full rundown below.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Thursday through Sunday
 
Thursday, September 24
Minooka

Heap's Giant Pumpkin Farm is open daily 10 AM–6:30 PM at 4853 US Highway 52. Weekday admission is $9 ages 3+ (2 & under free) for playgrounds, animals, corn maze, and the walking pumpkin path. Hayrides resume on the weekend. Hours & tickets.
 
Friday, September 25
Minooka / Channahon / Plainfield

Minooka at Plainfield North, varsity 7 PM at Plainfield North High School, 12005 S 248th Ave, Plainfield. Tickets on GoFan. IHSA schedule.

Fun Friday at Dollinger Family Farm, 10 AM–1 PM at 7420 E Hansel Rd, Channahon. Train rides and face painting before the weekend rush. Free admission; tickets for train and face painting on site. dollingerfarms.com.

Aces Friday Night Cruise at Aces Classic Cars, 502 Twin Rail Drive, from 4 PM (through early evening). Every Friday through October. acesclassiccars.com.

Sights of September Evening Paddle, 5–7 PM at McKinley Woods (Kerry Sheridan Grove). $20, ages 12+, prior kayaking experience required; equipment provided. Registration was required by today (call 815-722-9470). Will County Forest Preserve.

Sips, Sights & Sounds, 6–9 PM at Four Rivers Environmental Education Center, 25055 W Walnut Ln, Channahon. Village Arts & Culture Commission fundraiser with art auction, drinks, hors d'oeuvres, and live music. Ticketed. channahon.org.
 
Saturday, September 26
Minooka

Heap's pumpkin patch and hayrides at 4853 US Highway 52. Farm hours 10 AM–6:30 PM. Weekend admission $14 ages 3+ (2 & under free). Hayrides run about 10:30 AM–6 PM, weather permitting. Hours & tickets.
 
Sunday, September 27
Channahon / Minooka

Channahon Farmers Market, 9 AM–2 PM at Tractor Supply, 26829 W Eames St. Pet-friendly market with sourdough, eggs, produce, flowers, honey, and more. 815local.

MCHS Choir Boosters Car & Motorcycle Show, noon at Minooka Central Community High School, 301 S Wabena Ave. Spectator admission is free; food trucks, 50/50, and awards. Rain date Oct 11. Event details.

Heap's is open 10 AM–6:30 PM with hayrides again today. Same weekend admission as Saturday. heapsgiantpumpkinfarm.com.
GOOD TO KNOW

The recent Summit Park Concert in the Park (Time and Material) was cancelled for weather. No new public date posted yet. Watch the Village of Minooka Facebook for a reschedule.

U.S. 6 bridge repairs in Channahon are still underway with intermittent daytime lane closures over the DuPage River. Allow extra time. IDOT notice.
