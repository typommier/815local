FROM THE FRONT PORCH
Homecoming Friday, pumpkins and OktoBREWfest Saturday

Hey neighbor, tonight's free Concert in the Park at Summit Park was cancelled for weather (village is working on a reschedule). Friday is Homecoming: Minooka hosts Joliet West at Central Campus. Saturday Heap's pumpkin patch and hayrides open, and Shorewood's OktoBREWfest brings live music plus the rescheduled fireworks at Four Seasons Park. Full rundown below.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Thursday through Sunday
 
Thursday, September 17
Minooka

Time and Material Concert - CANCELLED (was 6–8 PM at Summit Park, 250 Rivers Edge Dr). Village of Minooka called it for weather and says they are working on a reschedule. It was set as free classic rock with Mel's Tacos and Iron&Glass Minooka. Watch the Village Facebook for a new date.
 
Friday, September 18
Minooka

Minooka hosts Joliet West for Homecoming. Sophomore game 5 PM; varsity 7:30 PM at Central Campus, 301 S Wabena St. Athletic Hall of Fame inductees are recognized after the sophomore game. Tickets on GoFan.

Aces Friday Night Cruise at Aces Classic Cars, 502 Twin Rail Drive, from 4 PM (through early evening). Every Friday through October. acesclassiccars.com.
 
Saturday, September 19
Shorewood / Minooka

Shorewood OktoBREWfest, 3:30–11:30 PM at Cene's Four Seasons Park, 25520 W Seil Rd. Free entry. German fare, local brews, live music (The Dirndolls 3:30, Don't Matter To Me 6 PM, How Rude! 9 PM). Fireworks at 8:30 PM (rescheduled from Crossroads Fest). Credit cards only; no chairs, pets, or outside food or drink. shorewoodil.gov · 815local.

Heap's pumpkin patch and hayrides open today at 4853 US Highway 52. Farm hours 10 AM–6:30 PM. Weekend admission $14 ages 3+ (2 & under free). Hayrides start with the patch opening. Hours & tickets · 815local.

Healthy Kids Running Series Fall Week 2, 3:30 PM at Walnut Trails Elementary, 301 Wynstone, Shorewood (Saturday this week). Same start time as OktoBREWfest, so pick what fits. healthykidsrunningseries.org.
 
Sunday, September 20
Minooka

Heap's is open 10 AM–6:30 PM with hayrides again today (weekend dates include Sep 19–20). Good day to grab a pumpkin if Saturday was packed. heapsgiantpumpkinfarm.com.

Channahon Farmers Market is off this Sunday; it returns Sep 27 at Tractor Supply.
GOOD TO KNOW

OktoBREWfest Saturday: expect heavier traffic and pedestrian activity around Four Seasons Park and Seil Road before, during, and after the 8:30 PM fireworks. Allow extra time and follow officer directions. Nearby homes and pets will hear the show. Village notice via Patch.

U.S. 6 bridge repairs in Channahon are underway (began Sep 10) with intermittent daytime lane closures over the DuPage River through about year-end. IDOT notice.
