FROM THE FRONT PORCH
A normal September weekend, finally

Hey neighbor, Labor Day is behind us. Thursday night is Rock On Channahon at Central Park. Friday Minooka hosts Yorkville at Central Campus. Shorewood's village-wide garage sale runs Friday and Saturday, and this is the last weekend for Heap's sunflower harvest (pumpkin season starts Sep 19). Full rundown below.
 
THIS WEEKEND
Thursday through Sunday
 
Thursday, September 10
Channahon / Minooka

Rock On Channahon, 6:30–8:30 PM at Central Park, 24856 W Eames St. Free outdoor concert from the Village, Park District, and Three Rivers Library. Bring chairs. channahon.org.

Village-wide garage sales also run today through Saturday in Minooka and Channahon (maps are posted). Shorewood's sale is Friday–Saturday (map).
 
Friday, September 11
Minooka / Shorewood

Minooka football hosts Yorkville, 7 PM varsity at Central Campus, 301 S Wabena St. Tickets on GoFan. Event details.

Shorewood Fall Village-Wide Garage Sale starts today (Friday–Saturday). shorewoodil.gov.

Shorewood Family Campout begins 5 PM at Shorewood Park, 1003 S Cottage St (overnight through Saturday 9:30 AM). Bring your own tent and gear. Pre-registration was required on RecDesk (enrollment closed Sep 8). RecDesk details.
 
Saturday, September 12
Minooka / Shorewood

Heap's sunflower harvest, 4853 US Highway 52, 10 AM–6:30 PM through Sunday Sep 13. Last days for the sunflower field; the farm stays open into pumpkin season. Tickets $12 ages 3+, weather and bloom dependent. Tickets / details.

Shorewood garage sales continue through Saturday.

Three Rivers Library Minooka: Urban Foraging walking tour, 10 AM–12 PM (meet at the branch). Register / details.
 
Sunday, September 13
Channahon / Shorewood / Minooka

Channahon Farmers Market, 9 AM–2 PM at Tractor Supply, 26829 W Eames St. Free, pet-friendly. Event details.

Healthy Kids Running Series (Fall Week 1), 3:30 PM at Walnut Trails Elementary, Shorewood. healthykidsrunningseries.org.

TRPL Minooka Gaming Days, 2–4 PM. Event details.

Last day for the sunflower harvest if the field is still blooming. Pumpkin patch and hayrides open Sep 19. Tickets.
GOOD TO KNOW

U.S. 6 bridge repairs in Channahon begin Thursday Sep 10 with intermittent daytime lane closures over the DuPage River (through about year-end). Plan extra time if you cross that bridge. IDOT notice.
